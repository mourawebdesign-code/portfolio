All photos are from Unsplash, under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use commercially, no permission or attribution legally required — credited below anyway as good practice). No paid/Unsplash+ (Getty) images were used.

## ⚠️ Before/after pairs are NOT real clinical photography

This is the single most important note in this file.

Every "Before" / "After" pair in **Treatments** and **Results** is two **different stock photos**, picked to share similar lighting, tone, and crop so they read as a coherent pair in the slider. They do **not** show the same person, and no real treatment produced the visual difference between them. This is standard practice for template/demo sites, but it must not go live as real clinic marketing — before shipping this to an actual business, replace every pair with real, consented, HIPAA-compliant patient photography, or drop the before/after mechanic for that treatment.

## Not used / rejected

Several other candidates were downloaded and reviewed but rejected during sourcing: a resort/Balinese-style spa interior (wrong tone — too dark/tropical vs. the site's clean lilac-and-white identity), a nail-salon pedicure room (wrong treatment category entirely), a black-and-white dramatic editorial portrait (inconsistent color/mood with the rest of the page), and a very graphic extreme macro of parted lips (read as unsettling out of context). None of these are referenced by the site.
