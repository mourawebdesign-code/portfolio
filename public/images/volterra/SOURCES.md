# Image sources — Volterra Electric

Most photos were provided directly by the client (custom photography/renders
with the Volterra Electric branding already applied) and dropped into this
folder. A few remaining images are free-to-use stock photography from Pexels,
downloaded individually (no hotlinking, no scraping). All images are
re-encoded to WebP locally. The company logo (`logo.webp`, used in the
navbar) and the hero character cutout (`/handyx/hero-worker.webp`, referenced
directly in `Hero.tsx`) were also client-provided.
